Back to the same few blocks of District 1 for the fifth stop in the
[hotel series](/categories/hotel-reviews/): **The White Hotel**, on **8A Thai
Van Lung Street** — the same micro-neighborhood as the
[Sunflower Luxury Hotel](/posts/sunflower-luxury-hotel-saigon/), and a useful
comparison if you're choosing between the two.

## The location: Japan Town and Ben Thanh within a short walk

Thai Van Lung Street puts you minutes from the Le Thanh Ton Japanese quarter,
with Ben Thanh Market and the Bui Vien nightlife zone both within walking
distance. One detail I appreciate: the well-known **Spa Linh Cherry is on the
same side of the road, a one-minute walk** — convenient for winding down
after a day of walking the city.

The hotel itself is a slim tower on a busy little street — classic District 1.

## Guest friendly: smooth, no fees, no theater

The filter that decides every booking in this series: **The White Hotel is
guest friendly**. A guest registers their ID at the front desk and joins you —
no joiner fee, no negotiation, no closed-circuit-door theatrics. If you're
new to this topic, start with [the La Paix review](/posts/la-paix-boutique-hotel-saigon/)
for the full background on why this one policy shapes the whole stay.

## The rooms and that breakfast

The rooms run a classic European style — carved gold accents, big comfortable
beds, a dressing table, and more character than the usual beige-box business
hotel. The hotel holds around **8.1/10 on Trip.com from 200+ reviews**, and
free WiFi plus an on-site restaurant and bar round out the basics.

The standout, though, is the **breakfast buffet** — a proper spread, not the
sad-toast minimum that budget hotels in this price bracket get away with.

## Verdict: White Hotel or Sunflower?

Both are guest-friendly, both flank Saigon's Japan Town, both are easy on the
wallet. The honest difference: **Sunflower** brings jacuzzi rooms and a
swimming pool; **The White Hotel** counters with the better breakfast, a more
characterful room, and often a lower rate. Pick by mood — I've stayed happy at
both. I'll keep this post updated if anything changes.
