Every journey starts somewhere, and this is mine: a blog with a single post,
written from a desk I intend to leave behind.

**Why this blog exists.** I've decided to build a digital nomad life — not the
Instagram version with infinity pools, but the real one: sustainable remote
income, a lean set of tools, and the freedom to choose where I wake up. This
site is where I'll document that process, the way I wish someone else had
documented it for me.

**What you'll find here.** Practical notes on the infrastructure of a nomadic
life — how I set up this very blog for zero cost, the tools I use to run work
from anywhere, money and tax lessons learned the hard way, and honest progress
reports including the failures.

**How it's built.** Fittingly for a blog about nomadic infrastructure, the
site itself is a lesson in doing more with less: [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/)
generates the static pages, [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/) hosts
them for free, and [Pages CMS](https://pagescms.org/) gives me a visual
editing panel in the browser. No servers, no databases, no monthly bills.

The road begins here. Next stop: the first real chapter.
