Hi, I'm **Laowang**.

*The Digital Nomad Way* is my personal journal about building a
location-independent life — working remotely, traveling slowly, and figuring
out what actually works versus what only looks good on social media.

I started this blog to keep myself honest: writing down the decisions, the
mistakes, and the small wins as I move from a fixed desk to a life where my
office can be anywhere. Expect practical notes on tools and infrastructure,
honest numbers when I can share them, and the occasional rant about visas and
wifi.

If any of that sounds familiar, come along. The road is long, but it's better
with company.

You can reach me through [GitHub](https://github.com/bjachlxam).
